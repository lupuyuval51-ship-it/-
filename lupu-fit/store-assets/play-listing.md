# Google Play store listing (Device Mode build)

Text for Play Console > Grow > Store presence > Main store listing. Hebrew is the default language
(Play code `iw-IL`), English (`en-US`) is the translation. Paste the text inside each fence as is.

Limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4000 characters. The count after each heading is
the measured length of the fenced text in Unicode code points (what Play counts), and
`scripts/play-listing.test.ts` fails if a count is wrong, a limit is exceeded, or a content number below no
longer matches the content files.

What the text is based on (keep it true when the build changes):
- The Play build is Device Mode without an AI proxy (`client/.env.device` sets only `VITE_RUN_MODE=device`).
  The coach is the rules coach; photo calorie estimates and "more languages" answer `ai_unavailable`.
  If a build ever sets `VITE_AI_PROXY_URL`, rewrite the coach paragraph and the data safety answers
  (docs/ANDROID.md) first.
- No offline claim: finishing a workout needs a connection (`live.finishNeedsConnection`, `LiveSession.tsx` `finish()`).
  Say "works offline" only after Finish works offline in Device Mode.
- Barcode lookup is a direct HTTPS request from the WebView to `world.openfoodfacts.org` (`ALLOWED_HOSTS` in
  `client/src/device/deviceWorker.ts`, `server/src/routes/nutrition.ts`) and needs internet. The request carries the
  barcode number and no user identifiers, but Open Food Facts also sees the IP address and the WebView User-Agent.
- No export claim: on Android, `BackupPanel` export uses an anchor download (`downloadJson`), which writes no file in the
  Capacitor WebView, and `navigator.share` is missing there. Mention export only after Android writes a real file.
- Dish photos are AI-generated illustrations (`nutrition.menu.photoNote`); exercise clips are schematic
  animations generated by code and not reviewed by a coach (`reviewed:false`, D10).
- Reminders are local, off by default, with inexact timing (`automation.note.native`). Real delivery is not verified
  (PROGRESS.md), so the text promises no delay bound.
- XP comes from workouts, sets, PRs, the weekly goal, food-log days, protein days and weigh-ins, with daily, weekly and
  per-session caps (`XP` and the award functions in `shared/src/gamification.ts`; `server/src/routes/food.ts`,
  `server/src/routes/weights.ts`).
- The rules coach answers from the snapshot in `buildSnapshot` (`server/src/routes/coach.ts`): recent workouts, PRs,
  7-day food, weight trend, readiness and today's plan day. The text names only workouts, food and weight.
- Content counts: 244 exercises, 376 ready workouts, 500 dishes, 39 sport finishers (`validate-content`).
- Ages 13 and up (`LIMITS.age.min`); under 18: no calorie deficit, RPE at most 7, no 1RM estimates.

Before uploading: the 16 screenshots in `store-assets/screenshots/` are dated Nov 1, before the choice-card UI
commit `efa1c64`. Retake them if the screens they show changed (docs/UX_GUIDE.md "How you work").

## English (en-US)

### Title (max 30): 30 characters
```text
LUPU-FIT: Strength & Nutrition
```

### Short description (max 80): 73 characters
```text
Strength workouts, nutrition targets and progress, stored on your device.
```

### Full description (max 4000): 1938 characters
```text
LUPU-FIT is a strength training and nutrition app in Hebrew and English. It keeps your data on your phone.

Workouts
- Workouts fitted to where you train, the equipment you have, your available time, injuries you report and your age.
- 244 exercises, each with a schematic animated illustration of the movement. The illustrations are generated by code, not filmed.
- 376 ready workouts, 39 sport finishers of about 10 minutes (general conditioning, not sport-skill training), or a plan built from your profile.
- Workout screen with set logging and a rest timer.

Nutrition
- Daily targets for calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat, based on your profile.
- Daily menus from 500 dishes. Dish photos are AI-generated illustrations, not the actual meal. Nutrition values are calculated from the ingredients.
- Food log with search, quick add and barcode lookup. Barcode lookup uses Open Food Facts and needs an internet connection. It sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts; like any web request, it also reveals your IP address.

Progress
- Progress, personal records and XP for workouts, food logging and weigh-ins, with daily and weekly caps.
- Weight log with a trend.

Reminders
- Optional local reminders for workout days, meals and weigh-ins, off by default. Reminders are not exact and can arrive later than the time you set.

Coach
- The built-in coach answers with fixed rules, from what you log: workouts, food and weight. An AI coach is available only when the app is connected to a configured AI server, and this version is not connected to one. Photo calorie estimates and translation into more languages are therefore not available in it.

Privacy
- Your data stays on your phone. No account e-mail, no ads and no trackers.

Safety
- General fitness information, not medical advice.
- For ages 13 and up. Under 18 the app applies gentler rules: no calorie deficit, effort capped at RPE 7 and no one-rep-max estimates.
```
